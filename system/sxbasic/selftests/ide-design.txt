# Savan BASIC's form designer driven with no window (sxbide --selftest; docs/userland/SXBASIC_IDE.md):
# a new project, its form made bigger, a label and a button drawn, the button's
# caption set in the grid, moved and resized, a check box drawn and deleted,
# then a double-click on the button writes its Click handler, which the form
